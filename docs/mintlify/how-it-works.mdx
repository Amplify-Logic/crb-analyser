# The CRB Analysis Pipeline

ReadyPath runs a 5-phase AI agent pipeline that mirrors the work of a senior technology consultant, but delivers in hours instead of weeks.

## Phase 1: Discovery

The adaptive quiz and workshop gather your business context:

- **Industry and company profile** — size, revenue, growth stage
- **Current technology stack** — tools, integrations, pain points
- **Team capabilities** — technical skills, capacity, training needs
- **Business goals** — what you're trying to achieve and by when
- **Constraints** — budget, compliance, timeline, vendor lock-in

## Phase 2: Research

AI agents research your specific context:

- **Vendor pricing** — real, current pricing from vendor websites and databases
- **Industry benchmarks** — how similar companies have solved the same problems
- **Integration compatibility** — which tools work with your existing stack
- **Risk factors** — known issues, vendor stability, migration complexity

## Phase 3: Analysis

The CRB framework evaluates every finding across three dimensions:

| Dimension | What's Measured |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Cost** | Financial, Time, Opportunity, Complexity, Risk, Brand/Trust |
| **Risk** | Technical, Adoption, Vendor, Market — each with probability and impact |
| **Benefit** | Financial, Time savings, Strategic value, Quality improvement |

Each finding gets a **NET Score**: `Benefit - Cost - (Risk / 10)`

## Phase 4: Modeling

For every recommendation, three implementation options are modeled:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Off-the-Shelf** | Existing SaaS tools. Lowest risk, fastest deployment, limited customisation. |
| **Best-in-Class** | Premium solutions with deeper integration. Higher cost, better fit. |
| **Custom Build** | Purpose-built solution. Highest initial cost, maximum flexibility. |

Each option includes full cost projections, risk assessment, and ROI timeline.

## Phase 5: Report

The final report is assembled and human-reviewed:

- **Executive Summary** — key findings and top recommendation
- **AI Readiness Score** — 0-100 across 5 weighted dimensions
- **Tiered Findings** — Quick Win, Strategic, Consider, Defer
- **Implementation Roadmap** — 3 phases with dependencies and milestones
- **Connect vs Replace** guidance for each recommendation

<Note>
  Every report is reviewed by a human expert before delivery to ensure accuracy, relevance, and actionability.
</Note>

## Timeline

| Step | Duration |
|------|----------|
| Quiz | 5-7 minutes |
| Workshop | 90 minutes |
| AI Analysis | 2-4 hours |
| Human Review | 4-8 hours |
| **Total** | **24-48 hours** |

Compare this to traditional consulting: 4-6 weeks, EUR 15,000+.
