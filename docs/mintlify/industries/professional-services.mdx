# CRB for Professional Services

Professional services firms face a unique technology challenge: their product is expertise, and technology must enhance — never replace — the human judgment that clients pay for.

## Common Technology Decisions We Analyse

| Decision | What CRB Evaluates |
|----------|-------------------|
| **AI-powered document review** | Cost of tools vs. time saved, accuracy risk, client acceptance |
| **Practice management systems** | Current tool fitness, migration cost, multi-location needs |
| **Client portal technology** | Build vs. buy, integration with existing systems, security |
| **Automated billing & reporting** | Time savings, error reduction, client experience impact |
| **Knowledge management** | Internal wiki vs. AI-powered search, adoption risk |
| **CRM & business development** | Off-the-shelf vs. industry-specific, data migration |

## Industry-Specific Considerations

### Compliance & Data Sensitivity
- Client confidentiality requirements
- Regulatory compliance (GDPR, industry-specific)
- Data residency and sovereignty
- Audit trail requirements

### Billable Time Economics
Professional services revenue is directly tied to time. Every technology assessment considers:
- Net effect on billable hours (automation frees time vs. training absorbs it)
- Impact on realisation rates
- Client-facing vs. internal efficiency

### Partnership Decision-Making
Technology decisions often require partner consensus. CRB reports are designed to:
- Present clear executive summaries for time-poor partners
- Show financial impact in terms partners understand (revenue per partner, utilisation rates)
- Provide evidence that satisfies the most skeptical decision-maker

## What Professional Services Firms Discover

Based on our analysis patterns, common findings include:

1. **Quick Win**: Automating document generation saves 5-15 hours/week per team
2. **Strategic**: AI-powered research tools reduce research time by 40-60%
3. **Consider**: Client portal upgrades improve NPS but require change management
4. **Defer**: Full practice management migration — often better to connect than replace
