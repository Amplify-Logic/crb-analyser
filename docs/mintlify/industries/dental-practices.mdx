# CRB for Dental Practices

Dental practices are technology-intensive businesses where the right software stack directly impacts patient experience, clinical outcomes, and profitability. But with hundreds of dental-specific tools available, making the right choice is overwhelming.

## Common Technology Decisions We Analyse

| Decision | What CRB Evaluates |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Practice management software** | Feature fit, migration cost, multi-location support |
| **Patient communication** | Automated reminders, online booking, patient portals |
| **Imaging & diagnostics** | AI-assisted analysis, integration with existing hardware |
| **Digital workflow** | Intraoral scanning, CAD/CAM, lab integration |
| **Revenue cycle management** | Insurance verification, claims processing, payment plans |
| **Marketing & patient acquisition** | Website, SEO, review management, social media tools |

## Industry-Specific Considerations

### Clinical Integration
- Tools must integrate with clinical workflows, not disrupt them
- Chair-side usability is critical — complex UIs cost clinical time
- Hardware compatibility (imaging, scanners) constrains software choices

### Multi-Location Complexity
Many dental organisations operate across multiple locations:
- Centralised reporting and scheduling
- Consistent patient experience across sites
- Shared vs. location-specific configurations

### Regulatory Requirements
- HIPAA compliance (US) or equivalent healthcare data regulations
- Patient data portability
- Audit and record-keeping requirements

### Vendor Landscape
The dental software market is fragmented with many niche players:
- CRB evaluates vendor stability and acquisition risk
- Integration capabilities between dental-specific tools
- True cost of ownership including training and support

## What Dental Practices Discover

Common findings from our dental practice analyses:

1. **Quick Win**: Automated patient reminders reduce no-shows by 20-30%
2. **Strategic**: Integrated practice management replaces 3-4 separate tools
3. **Consider**: AI-assisted imaging improves diagnostic confidence but requires training investment
4. **Defer**: Full digital workflow transition — phase based on volume and ROI
