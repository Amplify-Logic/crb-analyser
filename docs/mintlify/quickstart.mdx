# Get Your CRB Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the Quiz" icon="play">
    Navigate to [readypath.ai/quiz](https://readypath.ai/quiz) and answer 5-7 adaptive questions about your business and technology stack. The quiz adapts based on your answers — no two assessments are identical.

    You'll receive:
    - **AI Readiness Score** (0-100)
    - **Report teaser** with top-level findings
    - **Industry benchmarks** comparing you to peers
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlock Full Analysis" icon="lock-open">
    Purchase the full CRB report for **EUR 147**. This includes:
    - 90-minute AI-guided workshop
    - Comprehensive CRB report (24-48hr delivery)
    - Human expert review and validation
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Workshop" icon="comments">
    Join the 90-minute workshop where our AI captures deep context about your:
    - Current technology stack and integrations
    - Team capabilities and capacity
    - Business goals and constraints
    - Pain points and opportunities
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Report" icon="file-chart-column">
    Within 24-48 hours, receive your human-reviewed CRB report with:
    - Prioritised findings (Quick Win / Strategic / Consider / Defer)
    - Three options per recommendation with full cost breakdowns
    - Implementation roadmap with phases and dependencies
  </Step>
</Steps>
