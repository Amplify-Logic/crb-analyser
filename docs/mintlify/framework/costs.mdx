# The 6 Cost Dimensions

CRB doesn't just look at license fees. We evaluate 6 categories of cost to capture the true price of every technology decision.

## 1. Financial Cost

Direct monetary costs including:

| Component | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| **Software licensing** | Monthly/annual SaaS fees, per-seat pricing |
| **Implementation** | Setup fees, data migration, custom development |
| **Training** | Staff training time, certification costs |
| **Ongoing maintenance** | Support contracts, upgrades, admin overhead |

All pricing sourced from current vendor data and verified against published rates.

## 2. Time Cost

The time investment required from your team:

- **Implementation timeline** — weeks/months to go live
- **Learning curve** — time to proficiency for each role
- **Migration effort** — data transfer, testing, parallel running
- **Ongoing admin** — hours per week for maintenance

Time cost is calculated as: `hours x average hourly rate x duration`

## 3. Opportunity Cost

What you can't do while pursuing this option:

- **Delayed projects** — other initiatives that get pushed back
- **Market timing** — competitive advantage lost during implementation
- **Resource allocation** — team capacity diverted from revenue-generating work

## 4. Complexity Cost

The hidden cost of adding complexity to your stack:

- **Integration burden** — APIs, data sync, middleware
- **Vendor dependencies** — lock-in, API changes, sunset risk
- **Technical debt** — workarounds, customisations, compatibility layers

## 5. Risk Cost

The expected cost of things going wrong (detailed in [Risk Analysis](/framework/risks)):

```
Risk Cost = Probability x Financial Impact
```

## 6. Brand/Trust Cost

Impact on your reputation and relationships:

- **Client-facing changes** — how transitions affect client experience
- **Data handling** — privacy implications of new tools
- **Reliability** — downtime risk during and after migration
