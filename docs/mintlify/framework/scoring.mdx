# Scoring Methodology

ReadyPath uses three scoring systems to quantify technology decisions.

## AI Readiness Score (0-100)

Your overall readiness for AI and technology adoption, measured across 5 weighted dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| **Current Stack** | 20% | Quality and modernity of existing tools |
| **Data Readiness** | 25% | Data quality, accessibility, and structure |
| **Team Capability** | 20% | Technical skills and capacity for change |
| **Process Maturity** | 15% | How well-defined and documented processes are |
| **Strategic Alignment** | 20% | How well technology goals align with business goals |

### Score Ranges

| Range | Rating | Interpretation |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| 80-100 | **Excellent** | Ready for advanced AI adoption |
| 60-79 | **Good** | Ready with some preparation needed |
| 40-59 | **Moderate** | Foundation building required first |
| 20-39 | **Developing** | Significant groundwork needed |
| 0-19 | **Early** | Start with basics before considering AI |

## NET Score

The decision-making score applied to each recommendation option:

```
NET SCORE = Total Benefit - Total Cost - (Total Risk / 10)
```

| Component | Source |
|-----------|--------|
| Total Benefit | Sum of Financial + Time + Strategic + Quality benefits (confidence-adjusted) |
| Total Cost | Sum of all 6 cost categories over evaluation period |
| Total Risk | Sum of (Probability x Impact) across all risk categories |

**The option with the highest NET score is the recommended choice.**

### Example

| | Off-the-Shelf | Best-in-Class | Custom Build |
|-|---------------|---------------|--------------|
| Benefit | EUR 45,000 | EUR 72,000 | EUR 95,000 |
| Cost | EUR 12,000 | EUR 35,000 | EUR 65,000 |
| Risk / 10 | EUR 800 | EUR 1,500 | EUR 3,200 |
| **NET Score** | **EUR 32,200** | **EUR 35,500** | **EUR 26,800** |

In this example, **Best-in-Class wins** — higher benefits justify the extra cost, and risk is manageable.

## Customer Value Score (0-10)

Measures the value delivered to your clients and customers:

- Impact on service quality
- Client experience improvement
- Competitive differentiation
- Revenue growth potential

## Business Health Score (0-10)

Measures impact on operational sustainability:

- Operational efficiency gains
- Risk reduction to the business
- Team satisfaction and retention
- Long-term cost trajectory

## Dual-Pillar Validation

Every recommendation must score positively on **both** Customer Value and Business Health:

| Customer Value | Business Health | Decision |
|---------------|----------------|----------|
| High | High | **Recommend** |
| High | Low | Caution — unsustainable |
| Low | High | Caution — limited client impact |
| Low | Low | **Do not recommend** |

<Note>
  CRB rejects recommendations that only benefit one pillar. A cost-cutting measure that hurts client experience, or a client-pleasing feature that damages margins, will be flagged.
</Note>
