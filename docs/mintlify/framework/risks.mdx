# Risk Assessment Framework

Risk in CRB analysis is not a vague "red/yellow/green" rating. Every risk is quantified with probability, impact, and concrete mitigation strategies.

## 4 Risk Categories

### Technical Risk

Will the technology work as expected?

| Factor | What We Assess |
|--------|---------------|
| **Integration complexity** | API compatibility, data format mismatches, middleware needs |
| **Performance** | Scalability limits, latency, throughput constraints |
| **Reliability** | Uptime history, failure modes, disaster recovery |
| **Security** | Vulnerability history, compliance certifications, data handling |

### Adoption Risk

Will your team actually use it?

| Factor | What We Assess |
|--------|---------------|
| **Learning curve** | Time to proficiency, training requirements |
| **Change resistance** | Workflow disruption, habit changes required |
| **Champion dependency** | Risk of adoption failing if key advocates leave |
| **Parallel systems** | How long you'll run old and new systems together |

### Vendor Risk

Will the vendor be there when you need them?

| Factor | What We Assess |
|--------|---------------|
| **Financial stability** | Funding status, revenue model, burn rate |
| **Product direction** | Roadmap alignment, feature sunset history |
| **Lock-in degree** | Data portability, API dependency, export options |
| **Support quality** | Response times, escalation paths, documentation |

### Market Risk

Will the landscape shift under you?

| Factor | What We Assess |
|--------|---------------|
| **Technology evolution** | Risk of the approach becoming obsolete |
| **Competitive dynamics** | What competitors are adopting |
| **Regulatory changes** | Upcoming compliance requirements |
| **AI disruption** | How AI developments may change the equation |

## Scoring Method

Each risk is scored on two dimensions:

| Dimension | Scale | Meaning |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **Probability** | 0-100% | Likelihood of the risk materialising |
| **Impact** | 1-10 | Severity if it does occur |

**Risk Score** = `Probability x Impact`

This feeds into the NET Score formula as `Risk / 10`, ensuring high-risk options are penalised proportionally.

## Mitigation Strategies

Every identified risk includes a specific mitigation:

| Risk | Mitigation Example |
|------|--------------------|
| Integration fails | Proof-of-concept sprint before full commitment |
| Low adoption | Pilot with champion team, measure before rollout |
| Vendor instability | Contractual data portability clause, export schedule |
| Technology obsolescence | Modular architecture, avoid deep vendor coupling |

<Warning>
  ReadyPath surfaces risks that vendors don't advertise. If a "risk-free" claim sounds too good to be true, it usually is.
</Warning>
