## What You Get

Your CRB report includes:

- **AI Readiness Score** (0-100) across 5 weighted dimensions
- **Tiered Findings** prioritised by impact: Quick Wins, Strategic, Consider, and Defer
- **Three Options per Finding**: Off-the-Shelf, Best-in-Class, and Custom Build with full cost/risk/benefit breakdowns
- **NET Score** for each option: `Benefit - Cost - (Risk / 10)` so the best choice is obvious
- **Implementation Roadmap** with 3 phases, timelines, and dependencies
- **Connect vs Replace** guidance on integrating with your existing tools
