# The Adaptive Quiz

The ReadyPath quiz isn't a static questionnaire. It's an adaptive assessment that selects questions based on your previous answers to build the most accurate picture of your situation in the fewest questions.

## How It Works

1. **Start anonymously** — no account needed, just begin answering
2. **5-7 questions** adapt based on your responses
3. **Confidence scoring** — the quiz knows when it has enough signal
4. **Instant results** — AI Readiness Score and report teaser immediately

## What We Ask About

| Area | Example Questions |
|------|------------------|
| **Industry & size** | What sector? How many employees? |
| **Current tools** | What's your tech stack? Any pain points? |
| **Goals** | What are you trying to achieve in the next 12 months? |
| **Constraints** | Budget range? Compliance requirements? |
| **Team** | Technical capabilities? Capacity for change? |

The quiz engine selects from a larger question bank based on your answers, skipping questions it can infer and diving deeper where uncertainty remains.

## What You Get

### Immediately (Free)

- **AI Readiness Score** (0-100) across 5 dimensions
- **Industry benchmark** — how you compare to similar companies
- **Report teaser** — top 2-3 findings preview
- **Priority recommendation** — your single most impactful next step

### After Purchase (EUR 147)

- 90-minute deep-dive workshop
- Full CRB report with all findings and recommendations
- Human expert review and validation

Your quiz progresses through these stages:

```
Created → In Progress → Completed → Payment Pending → Paid →
Workshop Complete → Report Delivered
```

Each state transition is tracked so you can return and pick up where you left off.
