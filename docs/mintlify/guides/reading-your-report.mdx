# Reading Your CRB Report

Your report is structured to be actionable from page one. Here's how to get the most from each section.

## Report Structure

### 1. Executive Summary

The first page gives you:
- **AI Readiness Score** with dimensional breakdown
- **Top recommendation** — the single most impactful action
- **Key numbers** — total potential value, investment required, payback period

Start here. If you only read one page, this is it.

### 2. Strategic Overview

Context for the analysis:
- Your industry landscape and peer benchmarks
- Key themes from the quiz and workshop
- Overall technology posture assessment

### 3. Tiered Findings

Findings are grouped by priority:

| Tier | Meaning | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Quick Win** | High value, low effort | Start this week |
| **Strategic** | High value, needs planning | Start planning now |
| **Consider** | Moderate value, evaluate further | Review when capacity allows |
| **Defer** | Not yet viable or low priority | Revisit in 6-12 months |

### 4. Detailed Recommendations

Each finding includes:

- **Problem statement** — what's not working or missing
- **Three options** with full CRB breakdown ([learn more](/framework/three-options))
- **NET scores** — so the best option is mathematically clear
- **Connect vs Replace** guidance
- **Implementation notes** — specific steps, dependencies, timeline

### 5. Implementation Roadmap

A phased plan across three horizons:

| Phase | Timeline | Focus |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Phase 1: Foundation** | 0-3 months | Quick wins and preparation |
| **Phase 2: Build** | 3-6 months | Strategic implementations |
| **Phase 3: Optimise** | 6-12 months | Refinement and expansion |

Each phase lists specific actions, dependencies, and success criteria.

### 6. Appendix

Supporting data:
- Vendor comparison details
- Benchmark sources and methodology
- Confidence level explanations
- Glossary of terms

## How to Use NET Scores

The NET score is your decision tool:

```
NET SCORE = Benefit - Cost - (Risk / 10)
```

- **Compare options** — higher NET score = better risk-adjusted value
- **Compare findings** — higher NET score findings should get priority
- **Check confidence** — a high NET score with low confidence needs validation

<Tip>
  Don't just pick the highest NET score blindly. Read the risk section — sometimes a slightly lower NET score with much lower risk is the better choice for your situation.
</Tip>

## Next Steps After Reading

1. **Share with stakeholders** — the executive summary is designed for this
2. **Start with Quick Wins** — they build momentum and prove value
3. **Plan Strategic items** — use the roadmap as your starting point
4. **Revisit in 30 days** — we follow up to check progress and answer questions
