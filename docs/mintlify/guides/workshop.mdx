# The 90-Minute Workshop

The workshop is where CRB analysis goes from surface-level to deeply personalised. Our AI guides a structured conversation that captures everything needed for an accurate report.

## What to Expect

| Phase | Duration | Focus |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Context Setting** | 15 min | Business overview, goals, constraints |
| **Stack Deep-Dive** | 25 min | Current tools, integrations, pain points |
| **Opportunity Mapping** | 25 min | Where AI/technology can create value |
| **Priority Alignment** | 15 min | What matters most, budget, timeline |
| **Wrap-Up** | 10 min | Summary, next steps, report timeline |

## What We Capture

### Business Context
- Company size, revenue, growth trajectory
- Industry-specific challenges and regulations
- Competitive landscape and positioning

### Technology Stack
- Every tool currently in use
- Integration points and data flows
- What works well and what doesn't
- Previous migration experiences

### Team & Culture
- Technical skills across the organisation
- Appetite for change and innovation
- Decision-making process
- Training capacity

### Goals & Constraints
- Short-term priorities (next 6 months)
- Strategic objectives (1-3 years)
- Hard constraints (budget, compliance, timeline)
- Soft preferences (vendor preferences, risk tolerance)

## How It Informs the Report

Workshop insights directly shape:

- **Which findings are relevant** — we don't report on areas that don't apply to you
- **Risk calibration** — your team's actual capacity and experience
- **Option selection** — which vendors and approaches fit your constraints
- **Priority ranking** — findings are ordered by what matters to *you*, not generic best practices

<Tip>
  The more context you provide in the workshop, the more accurate and actionable your report will be. There are no wrong answers — we need the real picture, not the aspirational one.
</Tip>
