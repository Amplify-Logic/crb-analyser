# E-commerce Sales Lock-In Strategy (Ready to Sell)

> Status: Ready for execution
> Created: 2026-03-03
> Owner: Founder
> Domain lock: E-commerce only until lock criteria are met

---

## 1) Decision: Lock Domain Now

We are locking to **E-commerce** as the first go-to-market wedge.

### Why this wedge
- Fastest feedback loops on ROI and implementation.
- Clear recurring pain (support, returns, attribution, merchandising, inventory).
- Tool-heavy environments (Shopify ecosystem) where architecture and automation advice has immediate value.
- High density of decision-makers who can buy quickly (founder, head of ops, ecommerce lead).

### Anti-drift rule
- No new vertical positioning, messaging, feature roadmap, or outbound campaigns outside e-commerce.
- Any non-e-commerce request is either declined or fulfilled only if it does not consume roadmap cycles.

---

## 2) Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary ICP
- Revenue: EUR 500k to EUR 10M annual run rate.
- Team size: 5 to 30.
- Platform: Shopify (priority), WooCommerce secondary.
- Geography: Europe first (EUR pricing consistency), English-language ops.

### Buyer persona
- Founder / CEO (sub-5M brands).
- Head of Operations / Ecommerce Manager (5M+ brands).

### Trigger signals (buying intent)
- Hiring support ops while tickets still rising.
- Returns margin pressure.
- Attribution confusion and ad spend uncertainty.
- Tool sprawl (Klaviyo, Gorgias, apps) with weak integration.
- Manual reporting or KPI inconsistencies.

### Disqualifiers
- < EUR 250k revenue.
- No operational complexity yet (single channel, tiny SKU count, low volume).
- Wants "AI strategy deck only" with no implementation intent.

## 3) Core Offer and Pricing

## Offer stack

### Tier A: Self-Serve Readiness Check (existing)
- Price: EUR 147.
- Purpose: lead qualification + trust builder.
- Outcome: baseline readiness + teaser insights.

### Tier B: E-commerce AI Ops Audit (core sale)
- Launch price: EUR 497 (first 10 clients), then EUR 900 to EUR 1,500.
- Delivery window: 7 days.
- Core promise: top 3 ROI-ranked automations with stack-aware implementation path.

### Tier C: Implementation Sprint (upsell)
- Price: EUR 2,000 to EUR 5,000.
- Scope: 2-4 automations implemented or setup-ready in client environment.

## Audit deliverables (what they are buying)
- Current-state stack and process map.
- 10-15 opportunities scored with CRB logic.
- Top 3 prioritized opportunities with ROI, payback, and confidence.
- Vendor and build-path recommendation for each top opportunity.
- 30-day implementation roadmap.
- "Do not automate now" list with reasons.

## Guarantee framing (risk reduction)
- "If we cannot identify at least 3 concrete opportunities with measurable value potential, you do not pay for implementation advisory."

## 6.3 Discovery call script (20 min)
- What is your #1 operational bottleneck this quarter?
- Where is margin leaking today?
- Which workflows are still highly manual?
- Current stack for support, CRM, email/SMS, analytics?
- What has already been tried and failed?
- If solved in 30 days, which KPI should move first?

Close:
"Based on this, we can likely deliver 3 high-confidence opportunities in 7 days. Want us to send a scope and fixed price?"

## 6.4 Objection handling
- "We can do this with ChatGPT."  
  "You can generate ideas with ChatGPT. We deliver prioritization with economics, implementation paths, and confidence-calibrated recommendations for your exact stack."

- "Too expensive."  
  "If one recommendation saves even 5-10 ops hours/week or reduces return leakage, the audit pays for itself quickly. We can start with the pilot tier."

- "No time right now."  
  "That is exactly when this helps. We remove tool-evaluation overhead and give a ranked execution plan your team can act on immediately."

## 6.5 Proposal skeleton (one page)
- Current pain summary.
- Audit scope and deliverables.
- Timeline and checkpoints.
- Price and payment terms.
- Success criteria and next-step call date.

## 7) Delivery SOP (7 Days)

## Day 0: Kickoff
- Confirm goals, constraints, and success KPI.
- Collect stack access/context and existing reports.

## Day 1-2: Discovery and baseline
- Review current workflows and tool topology.
- Quantify baseline pain where possible.

## Day 3-4: Analysis and prioritization
- Generate and score opportunities.
- Build top 3 recommendations with ROI and risk.

## Day 5: Draft report and QA
- Validate assumptions and data quality.
- Ensure actionable implementation steps.

## Day 6: Finalization
- Prepare executive summary + roadmap.
- Prepare client walkthrough narrative.

## Day 7: Delivery call + upsell transition
- Present recommendations and decision paths.
- Align on implementation sprint scope.
