## ICP Guardrail (Do Not Break)

Only pursue leads that match:
- EUR 500k to EUR 10M annual revenue
- Shopify or WooCommerce
- 5 to 30 team
- clear ops pain (support, returns, attribution, inventory, reporting)

If not matched, log and deprioritize.

## Message Discipline

### Core line (always)
"We identify which e-commerce automations to do first, ranked by ROI and implementation effort for your current stack."

### Never say
- "AI transformation"
- "Cutting-edge platform"
- "General strategy"

### Always include
- one clear pain
- one clear outcome
- one clear CTA (20-min call)

---

## Delivery QA Checklist (Before Every Client Delivery)

- Top 3 recommendations are specific and executable
- ROI assumptions are explicit and realistic
- "Do not do now" section is included
- 30-day plan is sequenced by impact and dependencies
- Narrative matches buyer role and business goals
