## 2. The Business Model

### What You're Selling

```
FREE QUIZ (3 min)
    ↓
PAYWALL at €147
    ↓
90-MIN WORKSHOP (voice/chat, AI-facilitated)
    ↓
HUMAN-REVIEWED REPORT (24-48 hour delivery)
```

### Why This Flow Works

| Stage | Purpose | Your Effort |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Quiz | Qualify leads, build curiosity | None (automated) |
| Workshop | Deep context gathering, build trust | ~20 mins review |
| Report | Deliver massive value, generate referrals | ~2-3 hrs per report |

**At €147 per report and 2-3 hours of your time = €49-73/hour effective rate.**

With experience, you'll get faster. And the data from each report makes the next one better.

### Pricing Rationale

| Option | Price | Why Not |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Free | €0 | No revenue, no commitment from customer |
| €47 | Low | Feels like a gimmick, attracts tire-kickers |
| €97 | Medium | Still feels "cheap" for business advice |
| **€147** | Sweet spot | Impulse-buy for SMB, signals real value |
| €197+ | Premium | May reduce volume early on |

Start at €147. Raise to €197 when you have testimonials and confidence.

## 3. Customer Acquisition Strategy

### Phase 1: Proof of Concept (First 10 Reports)

**Goal:** Deliver 10 reports to real home services businesses across your markets. Get testimonials.

| Market | Source | Target |
|--------|--------|--------|
| NZ | Friendly Build & Fix | 1 (free pilot) |
| NZ | Their network | 2-3 referrals |
| NL | Your network | 2-3 trades you know |
| UK/AU | LinkedIn outreach | 2-3 cold |

**Key message:**
> "I help small building/plumbing/electrical businesses figure out which AI tools are worth their time - and which to skip. 15-minute quiz, then I send you a personalised report with specific tools, prices, and a week-by-week implementation plan."

### Phase 2: Content + Referrals (Reports 11-50)

Once you have testimonials:

1. **Case studies** - "How Friendly Build & Fix saved 12 hours/week"
2. **LinkedIn posts** - Share insights from your reports (anonymised)
3. **Industry groups** - NZ building/trades Facebook groups, forums
4. **Referral ask** - "Know another builder who's drowning in admin?"

**Don't spend on ads yet.** Validate with organic first.

### Phase 3: Partnerships (Optional, Later)

- Trade software vendors (Fergus, Tradify) - they want customers to succeed
- Trade associations (Master Builders NZ, Plumbers Association)
- Accountants who serve trades

But this is LATER. First: 10 reports, 3 testimonials.

## 4. Delivery Process

### Your Weekly Rhythm

```
MONDAY-FRIDAY
├── Morning: Check for new quiz completions
├── Midday: Review workshop transcripts, flag questions
├── Afternoon: Generate + review reports
└── Evening: Send completed reports, gather feedback

WEEKEND: Off (or catch up if behind)
```

### Per-Report Workflow

| Step | Time | What Happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| Quiz completed | - | Auto-generates teaser, triggers payment |
| Payment received | - | Opens workshop access |
| Workshop completed | 10 min | Review transcript, note key details |
| Generate report | 15-30 min | AI generates draft, you review |
| Quality check | 30-60 min | Verify vendors, adjust recommendations |
| Send to customer | 5 min | Email with report link + personal note |
| Follow-up | 10 min | 1 week later: "Any questions?" |

**Total: 1-2 hours per report** (with practice)

### Quality Checklist (Before Sending)

- [ ] Company name correct throughout
- [ ] Industry/location appropriate vendors
- [ ] ROI calculations make sense
- [ ] Playbook tasks are actionable (not vague)
- [ ] "Not recommended" section exists
- [ ] No obvious AI hallucinations in vendor pricing

## 6. What NOT To Do

### Don't Build
- Mobile app
- White-label version
- Team collaboration features
- API for integrations
- Second industry support

**Until** you have 50+ paid reports in home services.

### Don't Market To
- Enterprise companies (slow, complex)
- Very small solopreneurs (no budget)
- Companies outside home services (stay focused)

### Don't Spend On
- Paid ads (before organic works)
- Fancy branding/website redesign
- Tools you don't need yet
- Contractors/employees (stay solo)
