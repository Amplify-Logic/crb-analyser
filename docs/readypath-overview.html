The Customer Journey

From first touch to delivered report in 48-72 hours.

1. Company URL

Customer enters website. AI scrapes business signals, tech stack, team size.

2. Free Quick Quiz

5-7 questions. Delivers AI Readiness Score + top opportunities. Free value upfront.

3. Paywall: €147

To unlock full report, they pay. This funds the deep analysis.

4. 90-Minute AI Workshop (Required)

Deep-dive conversation with AI. Maps workflows, pain points, current tools, decision criteria. This is mandatory - no report without it. Quality of output directly correlates to depth of input.

5. AI Generation (~30 min)

Claude Opus analyzes everything. Queries knowledge bases. Generates full CRB analysis.

6. Code Validations

ROI math verified. Vendor pricing checked. Benchmark sources confirmed. Catches hallucinations.

7. Human Review

Domain expert reviews before delivery. Catches edge cases. Adds judgment.

8. Delivery: 48-72 hours

Customer gets login credentials. Live report platform. Implementation tracking begins.

Gold in, gold out. The AI makes assumptions when information is missing - which causes mistakes. The workshop ensures we have accurate, complete context before generating recommendations.

The Questions We're Really Asking

Honest assessment of the opportunity and risks.

1. Can this generate significant revenue?

€147 × 1,000 reports = €147K/year. Modest. But doable with solo founder + AI leverage.

€147 × 10,000 reports = €1.47M/year. Requires marketing spend, but margins stay high.

Upsells: Implementation support, premium advisory, agency referral fees could 3-5x per customer.

The question: Is €147 the right entry point? Does the funnel convert?

2. Is this defensible against AI improvements?

Risk: ChatGPT/Claude get so good anyone can do this analysis. Product becomes commoditized.

Potential moats: Vertical knowledge bases that compound. Implementation data from real customers. Trust/brand in specific niches. Human review layer.

Counter-argument: We ride the AI wave - better models make our output better too. Knowledge + data + process is the moat, not the AI itself.

Honest answer: Real risk. We need to compound faster than generic AI catches up.

3. Is this worth the time?

If it works: Scalable, high-margin, AI-leveraged business. Solo founder can run profitably.

If it doesn't: We've built knowledge bases, expertise system, learned what SMBs need. Foundation for next thing.

Opportunity cost: What else could this time/energy build? Is this the highest-leverage bet?
